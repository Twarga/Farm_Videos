## Product truth

Unknown stays Not published or Confirm at checkout. Public facts come from the current snapshot. Every price/rating has its actual source/check date. A terms review is not a provider performance certification. A supported 36-month estimate never includes guessed renewal/setup/tax/IP costs. Paid links never change rank; provider visits use /go/[id]. No invented urgency, stock, expiry or live claims.

## Voice

Short sentences; numbers before adjectives; plain explanations of billing and evidence. Prefer Visit [provider], Not published, due today, renews at, checked and The catch. Do not claim best, #1, exclusive, unbeatable, live price or an independent performance benchmark without proof. Proper product/provider names and the approved reference copy are not permission to invent unsupported claims. Guides and legal copy remain reviewed content, not mockup filler.

## Third-party marks

Provider marks identify inventory and are not endorsements. Retain source/license records and safe fallbacks. Do not assume mockup approval grants a trademark agreement or Trustpilot brand license: preserve dated rating attribution and source links, and review any third-party logo/star treatment before release. Public assets never carry draft private evidence or secrets.

## Motion and layout

Follow the reference layouts and meaningful motions with reduced-motion and hidden/offscreen pause. No art over prices/forms; no characters in table cells or admin. Share accessible controls, clear focus and data-state vocabulary across routes. No Mockup panel or rmToggle review tool ships.

## Delivery boundary

The product is being prepared as a tested, production-ready V1 release candidate. Deployment, live configuration changes and owner launch sign-off are a separate, later operation.
