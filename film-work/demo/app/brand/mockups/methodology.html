How we check · Method v1 · 31 Aug 2026

Receipts for every number.

We read each provider's own pricing page, not its ads. We turn every plan into one comparable number and write down the catch. The formula below is the whole method. Nothing is hidden, and commission never touches it.

01

Order never uses money.

Affiliate status, commission and partnerships are never inputs to rank. A test fails the build if they are.

02

Unknown stays unknown.

Missing facts say "Not published". Never zero, never "included".

03

Every fact is dated.

Prices and review scores are snapshots with a check date and a source link.

04

Links are labelled.

"Affiliate link" or "Direct link" sits next to every Visit button.

The 3-year real price

The ad price hides the term, the intro period and the renewal. So we price the first 36 months the way the provider's own terms describe them, then divide by 36.

real = ( due_today + Σ renewals + Σ required_extras ) ÷ 36

Prices in euros or pounds are converted to USD at the ECB rate on the check date, shown under the price. VAT treatment is stated per provider.

How "Value" is ordered

The default sort is the plain 3-year real price. The optional Value sort weighs four public inputs:

Real price (lower is better)45%
RAM per dollar25%
Trustpilot score, weighted by review count15%
Evidence confidence15%
