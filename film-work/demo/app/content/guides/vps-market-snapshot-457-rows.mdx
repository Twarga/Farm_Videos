## What the snapshot is useful for

Use the dated records to compare monthly cost, hardware, location, and reviewed catches. Check the evidence beside cash due and renewal terms; missing facts stay "Not published". Reviewers retain the source row and record why they exclude a plan or leave a field unknown.

## What it cannot tell you

This report cannot establish the cheapest provider in the market, future availability, real-world uptime, performance under your workload, tax-inclusive checkout cost, or legal suitability for your data. A provider may have plans outside the captured source, and a public row may change after the snapshot date.

Use the [current catalog](/catalog?sort=effective) to inspect the published records, read the [methodology](/methodology) for normalization and ranking boundaries, and open the provider source before purchase. The catalog is a decision aid, not a substitute for checking current terms.

The immutable source is `data/vps-offers-2026-08-31.csv`. The current counts come from the database, and the [catalog update log](/updates) shows snapshot dates and corrections. Run the repository's dataset QA command before making a new claim from the source CSV.
