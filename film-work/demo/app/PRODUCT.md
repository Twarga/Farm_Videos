# bestvpsforme.com

## Register
Product. Design serves the task: scan, filter, shortlist, leave. Homepage is a decision door, not a campaign.

## Purpose
Turn confusing VPS offers into a small honest shortlist: what fits, what it really costs, what the catch is, and why the recommendation can be trusted.

## Users
Beginner builders and practical self-hosters choosing a first or next VPS. They arrive with a job (n8n, Coolify, a site) and leave via Get deal. No account required.

## Personality
Calm, precise and human. The Shore belongs to the current TwargaOps crab/keeper world: a living scene around a readable comparison tool. Ubuntu Sans and Ubuntu Sans Mono, violet shell actions, lantern evidence and explicit caveats follow the owner-approved brand/shore/mockup. Art never exaggerates a fact.

## Anti-references
Dark gamer hosting templates. Affiliate listicles. Fake “live prices.” Unrelated magazine layouts or generic SaaS glow. Decoration presented as evidence, fabricated performance claims, commission-led ordering and any redesign that drifts from the approved Shore reference.

## Principles
- Rankings never use commission.
- Affiliate is labeled next to the commercial link.
- Unknown is shown as unknown.
- Catalog can be a table. Cards are optional, not the default.
