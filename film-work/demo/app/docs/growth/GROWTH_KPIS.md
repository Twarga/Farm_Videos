# Weekly growth scorecard

Review the last 7 and 30 days in the admin analytics view. Compare against the previous period; do not chase a single noisy day.

| KPI | Definition | First decision threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Catalog engagement | Sessions using search, filters, compare, or an offer page ÷ catalog sessions | Investigate a 20% week-over-week drop |
| Offer CTR | Offer clicks ÷ offer page views | Investigate below 8% |
| Outbound rate | Outbound clicks ÷ catalog/offer views | Investigate a 20% week-over-week drop |
| Conversion rate | Confirmed conversions ÷ outbound clicks | Treat as directional until 30+ clicks |
| EPC | Commission ÷ outbound clicks | Compare by provider and placement, never rank by it |
| Correction volume | Sourced corrections received per week | High volume means data quality work, not content deletion |

Weekly note: write one observation, one action, and one thing to leave alone. Keep commission and partner status in the commercial section; editorial ranking remains independent.
