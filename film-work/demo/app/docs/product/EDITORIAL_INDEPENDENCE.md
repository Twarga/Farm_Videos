# Editorial independence rule

bestvpsforme may earn a commission when a visitor chooses a labelled affiliate link. That commercial relationship is separate from the recommendation.

## What determines a recommendation

Organic catalog ordering uses published offer facts: effective monthly cost, hardware, workload fit, freshness, confidence, and the documented Trustpilot snapshot treatment. Affiliate availability, commission rate, cookie length, partner status, and sponsorship never enter the score.

## Provider relationships

- A provider can ask for a correction or apply for a partnership, but it cannot buy a position.
- Paid placement must be labelled and kept outside organic ranking.
- A correction needs a source URL and evidence review, whether or not the provider is a partner.
- If a relationship creates a conflict, record it in the admin audit log and keep the factual offer record independent.

These rules are summarized publicly on `/methodology` and are reviewed whenever the ranking code or commercial schema changes.
