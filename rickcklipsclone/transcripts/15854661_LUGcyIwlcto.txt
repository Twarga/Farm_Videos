The man only had $10 to his name, but ordered the most expensive steak on the menu. As soon as the waiter left, he took off his sunglasses and checked his surroundings. Soon, the $200 steak was brought to his table. He began carefully examining it and used his finger to feel the internal temperature of the steak. After that, he stared into the waiter's soul while shaking his head in disapproval. He exclaimed that the steak was ice cold. The waiter was shocked, but still had to comply with the guests so he took the steak back. However, when the second steak arrived, the man didn't even look at it before immediately sending it back. Turns out he wasn't here just for a free meal. He had a bigger plan. The man continued sending the steaks back until the head chef got so frustrated that he decided to just cook the steak himself. Fearing the man might be a famous food critic, he used all 30 years of experience to create the most beautiful steak ever seen, but once again, the man did not approve. The waiter didn't know what to do, so he called the head chef over. Immediately, the man began pointing out multiple flaws with the steak. The chef felt insulted. He wanted nothing but to kick the man out. Just then, the man said he was a chef himself, and if they let him into the kitchen, he would show them how it's done. Sick of the man's complaining, the head chef actually agreed. Turns out the man was never a chef and has only worked at McDonald's before, but using his knowledge, he started preparing the pastry. He brushed it with egg wash before placing the beef and not knowing what to do, he placed three slices of American cheese onto it just like a burger. He drew some cool looking patterns on the top and soon enough, it was time to plate. He made a design with some barbecue sauce he found and added chilies for decoration. As soon as everyone tasted it, the head chef became even more angry. Turns out he had been fired on the spot and the man was instantly hired to be the new head chef.
