If you ever fake a number, there's a way to tell a human made it up. A man stole $2 million with fake checks, made every amount look totally random, and still got caught. But how? Well, picture a building with 19 floors. Step into the elevator and look at the buttons. Floors 2 through 9 each start with a different number, but floor 1 and 10 all the way up to 19 all start with a 1. That's more than half the buttons! Real numbers work like that elevator, so 1s show up way more than anything else. On top of that, real randomness is streaky. Flip a coin 100 times, and you'll usually get 6 heads in a row. His checks mostly began with a 7, 8, or 9, and he avoided doubles to make them look random. But truly random numbers are the opposite. So no matter how random you try to make yours, there's almost always a way to tell.
