ALL Types of Asses, Explained

There are 47 million asses on Earth right now. I'm not being rude — I counted (well, the Food and Agriculture Organization counted, but I'm taking credit). Some are donkeys. Some are the things donkeys sit on. This video covers both, because English is a beautiful, confusing mess.

We start with the OG donkey: 40 million years of evolution, domesticated in Eritrea, tougher than a horse, smarter than you think, and responsible for carrying basically all of human civilization on its back. Then we branch out — figurative asses (your coworker), literal asses (the 50kg of muscle you walk around with), tragic asses (scorpions that drop their own butts), and the biological nightmare fuel that is the bombardier beetle's 100°C chemical cannon.

From wild asses to lazy asses to "I'm sorry, it does WHAT?" — this is the complete taxonomy. Science says your rear is more interesting than you ever wanted to know.
