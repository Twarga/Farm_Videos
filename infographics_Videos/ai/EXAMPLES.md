# Annotated examples — why these openings work

Three real openings from the 13 videos, explained line by line.

---

## Example 1 — the water video (documentary style, 15:11)

**The first 30 seconds:**

> أكتوبر 2024. سد المسيرة، ثاني أكبر سد في المغرب، كان تقريباً خاوي.
> ما بقى فيه حتى 1%، غير 0.4% من الماء.
> وقبل هذا الشيء بعام، كانت الرباط والدار البيضاء على وشك يتقطع عليهم الماء
> من الروبيني. وباش ينقذهم، دفن المغرب تحت الأرض أنبوب عملاق ديال الفولاذ، طوله
> 67 كلم، وتسلى في حوالي 8 شهور.

**Translation:**
*October 2024. Al Massira dam, Morocco's second largest, was almost empty. Only
0.4% water left. A year before that, Rabat and Casablanca were about to lose their
water from the Bouregreg. To save them, Morocco buried a giant steel pipe, 67 km
long, and finished it in about 8 months.*

**Why it works:**

1. **Date first.** “October 2024.” No greeting, no logo. It feels like news.
2. **Named place.** “Al Massira dam.” The viewer can locate it.
3. **Contrast.** “Second largest” vs “0.4%”. That gap is the hook.
4. **Raise the stakes.** “Rabat and Casablanca were about to lose their water.”
   Now it is personal; those are the biggest cities.
5. **Concrete solution.** “A giant steel pipe, 67 km, 8 months.” Two numbers and a
   time span. The viewer can picture it.
6. **Curiosity gap.** “But the story did not stop here…” — the next line promises
   a surprise.

**Then, at 0:31, the early ask:**

> إذا عجبك الموضوع، دير لايك، واكتب لنا رأيك في التعليقات، واشترك في القناة،
> وشارك الفيديو مع أصحابك.

*If you like the topic, like, comment, subscribe and share.*

This is only 31 seconds in. The channel asks early, then continues the story.
Do not wait until the end.

## Example 2 — Nador port (mint style, 23:12)

**The first 30 seconds:**

> ميرسك، إمإسسي، وسيماسيجيم. هادو أكبر ثلاثة الشركات في العالم اللي كانت تنقل
> السلعة في البحر بالكونتينيرات. وفين ما تحل شي ميناء كبير في شي بلاد، كتلقى
> هاد الشركات كيتسابقوا باش ياخدوا فيه بلاصة. اليوم هاد الشركات الثلاثة كاملين
> عندهم بلاصة في الموانئ ديال المغرب. ميرسك كتسير محطات في طنجة المتوسط.
> وإمإسسي وسيماسيجيم دخلوا لميناء جديد في الريف قريب من مدينة الناظور. سميتو
> الناظور غرب المتوسط.

**Translation:**
*Maersk, MSC and CMA CGM — the three biggest container shipping companies in the
world. Wherever a big port opens in any country, these companies race to take a
place in it. Today all three have a place in Morocco's ports. Maersk runs
terminals in Tanger Med. MSC and CMA CGM entered a new port in the Rif, near
Nador. Its name is Nador West Med.*

**Why it works:**

1. **Three names.** Recognizable global brands. The viewer thinks “this is big”.
2. **A simple rule.** “Wherever a big port opens, these companies race in.”
3. **A turn to Morocco.** “Today all three have a place in Morocco's ports.”
4. **Specific proof.** Tanger Med, Nador, the Rif.
5. **The name reveal.** “Its name is Nador West Med.” This is the title of the
   video, saved for the end of the hook.

Notice what is **not** there: no “welcome”, no “today we will talk about”, no
long introduction. The first sentence is already a fact.

## Example 3 — the finance law (graph-paper style, 20:51)

**The first 45 seconds:**

> من فاتح يناير 2027 بزاف د الحوايج غادي تبدلوا في حياة المغاربة. الخلصة، المعاش،
> فاتورة الضوء، الدعم المباشر، الدعم ديال السكن، الرسوم على اللحم المستورد، ثمن
> ديال الطوموبيلات والسجائر، وعدد المناصب اللي غادي تفتح في المباريات. كل شيء
> مكتوب في وثيقة واحدة فيها 85 صفحة. مشروع قانون المالية 2027. في هذه الحلقة
> غادي نحطوه تحت المجهر ونشرحوه لك بالخشيبات. إذا كنتي أجير في القطاع الخاص ولا
> موظف القطاع العام، متقاعد، أرملة، شاب كيقلب على خدمة، أسرة…

**Translation:**
*From January 1, 2027, many things will change in Moroccans' lives: salary,
pension, electricity bill, direct aid, housing aid, tax on imported meat, car and
cigarette prices, and the number of public jobs. Everything is written in one
85-page document: the 2027 finance bill. In this episode we put it under the
microscope and explain it simply. If you are a private-sector worker, a
public-sector worker, a retiree, a widow, a young person looking for work, a
family…*

**Why it works:**

1. **A date.** January 1, 2027. A deadline.
2. **A list of real-life items.** Salary, pension, electricity, housing, meat,
   cars, cigarettes. Everyone finds something that touches them.
3. **A single object.** “One document, 85 pages.” A concrete thing to explain.
4. **A promise.** “We will put it under the microscope and explain it simply.”
5. **The audience call-out.** “If you are a worker, retiree, widow, young person
   looking for work, a family…” The viewer is named. This is the strongest hook
   pattern in the channel.

Use this pattern when the topic is a law, a budget, a reform, a report or a
document.

## What to imitate — and what not to

**Imitate:**
- the fact-first opening;
- the short sentences;
- the number + “يعني” pattern;
- the questions;
- the audience call-out;
- the early ask;
- the two-choice ending.

**Do not imitate:**
- their exact sentences or phrases;
- their footage, thumbnails or voice;
- their mistakes (auto-caption errors, missing punctuation, wrong names);
- their pace when it accelerates too much (153 wpm is too fast).
