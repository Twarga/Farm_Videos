# Reverse-Engineering «إنفوجرافيك عربي» — 13 Videos

How this was made: 16 timestamped frames per video, scene-cut detection, full Arabic
transcript analysis (hooks, dialect, phrases, pacing), audio loudness and voice-pitch
measurement, plus short frame-by-frame motion strips. All data is in this folder.

---

## 1. The short version

- 13 videos, 3h45m total, 1080p/30fps H.264 (one source only had 720p).
- **One male narrator** in all videos. Voice pitch ~99–125 Hz, average **~107 Hz**.
- **Speaking pace 122–137 words per minute**, average **~131 wpm**.
- **Loudness ~ -14 LUFS**, true peak ~ -0.9 dB (YouTube standard).
- **Darija narration + Modern Standard Arabic technical words.** Roughly 40% Darija
  markers, 60% MSA vocabulary. Never pure MSA, never heavy slang only.
- **Short sentences** (about 8–15 words), lots of **rhetorical questions** (5–31 per video).
- **Numbers everywhere**: 6–193 mentions per video. Money, percentages, years, quantities.
- **Subtle music bed** (about 22 dB below the voice) and almost no silence (0–12%).
- **No long logo intro.** Narration/visual starts at 0–0.3s in 12 of 13 videos.
- The channel uses **several visual templates** (6 formats, below), not one.
- **Thumbnails and titles are a separate, very consistent system** (big Arabic text,
  faces, flags, numbers, “secret/truth” wording).

## 4. Script formula (all 13 videos)

### The hook (first 10–20 seconds)
Every video starts with a **concrete, surprising fact** — not a greeting, not “welcome
back”. 10 of 13 put a date, number or place in the first two sentences.

Recurring hook types:
1. **Date + crisis:** “October 2024. The dam was almost empty — 0.4%.”
2. **Then vs now:** “In 2000 it was $42B; in 2025 it was $182B.”
3. **First/record:** “November 2018: first in Africa, 320 km/h.”
4. **Myth-busting:** “Many think Samir is a person. No, brother, it's an acronym.”
5. **Question:** “What is the 2027 finance law hiding from Moroccans?”
6. **Document/announcement:** “On the night of October 30, 2026, a royal statement…”
7. **Place + object:** “In Khouribga, a rock called phosphorite is extracted…”

### Body structure
- **Cold open** → fact/stake (0:00–0:30).
- **Context** → why now, what changed (“داكشي علاش…” = that's why).
- **3–6 numbered chapters** (visual chapter cards: 01, 02… or “الجزء الأول”).
- Each chapter: **question → number/map/comparison → explanation → transition**.
- **Turn/implications** → what it means for Moroccans, risks, scenarios.
- **Close** → answer the opening question, then CTA.

### Language
- **Darija for the voice, MSA for facts and technical terms.** Examples from the
  transcripts: “بزاف ديال”, “داكشي علاش”, “شنو مخبي”, “غادي”, “كيفاش”, “دابا”,
  mixed with “الميزانية”, “القطاع الخاص”, “المندوبية السامية للتخطيط”, “صندوق النقد
  الدولي”.
- Short sentences (8–15 words). Average ~10–11 words.
- Lots of questions: “واش عارف؟”, “شنو وقع؟”, “علاش؟”, “شكون اللي؟”, “فين؟”.
- Frequent connectors: **لكن/ولكن** (329 total), **يعني** (156), **دابا** (76),
  **من بعد** (101), **حيث/حيت** (84), **باش** (179), **غير** (148), **حتى** (228),
  **ماشي** (148), **راه** (33), **صافي** (21), **داكشي علاش** (15).
- Recurring vocabulary: متر مكعب، مليار درهم، مليون متر مكعب، الدار البيضاء، طنجة
  المتوسط، جلالة الملك، الملك محمد السادس، القطاع الخاص، بنك المغرب.
- Numbers on screen match numbers in the voice; say the number, then explain it.

### Outro formula (almost identical in every video)
- “اكتب لنا في التعليقات” (write in the comments)
- “شنو بغيتي تكون الحلقة الجاية؟” (what should the next episode be?)
- Offer **two specific next topics** (A or B).
- “دير لايك وشارك” / “تابع القناة” / “صيفط الفيديو لصاحبك…”
- “نتلاقاو في حلقة جاية” (see you next episode).
- Sometimes part 2 tease: “هذا هو الجزء الثاني… غتلقاوه دابا على الشاشة.”
- Sometimes a concrete goal: “باش نوصلوا مع بعض 2000 لايك.”

### Pacing
- ~130 wpm → about **2,200 words for a 15-minute video** (measured median 2,202 words /
  12,783 characters; mean 2,279 / 13,539).
- 5–31 questions per video, 6–193 numbers per video.
- Long visual holds (25–500s per card in the infographic formats) — the visuals breathe
  and let the narration carry the scene.

## 5. Visual system

### Layout
- 1920×1080, 30fps, H.264.
- Arabic RTL, text right-aligned or centered.
- One main idea per screen; 3–5 elements max.
- Section label top-right with a small dot; channel watermark top-left or bottom-left.
- Source credit in small text at the bottom (e.g., “المصدر: Africa Intelligence”,
  “2018 © Aljazeera Copyrights, Sentinel-2”).

### Colors (sampled from the videos)
- Mint background: **#D7E6E6**, lighter band **#BCD1D1**
- Deep teal text: **#093532** / **#0E423B**
- Yellow accent: **#FDCA3B**
- Paper background: **#EAE4D5**
- Paper teal: **#0D7673**
- Graph paper: **#ECF0E9** / **#D7E2DB**
- Green: **#1C9639** / **#0D7345**
- Red: **#B71F22**
- Dark charcoal: **#262B2E**
- Lime: **#D5FE3D**

### Type
- Bold geometric Arabic sans, similar to **Cairo / Tajawal / Almarai**.
- Headline 60–90 px, body 36–48 px, source 20–24 px (at 1080p).
- Yellow marker/highlight behind the key phrase, not under the whole sentence.
- Numbers: big, often white on teal or dark teal on yellow.

### Recurring scene types (reuse these 10)
1. **Hook/stat card** — one big number + short line.
2. **Map scene** — Morocco outline or satellite; route line, pins, highlighted regions.
3. **Chapter card** — huge number + chapter title, wipe/slide transition.
4. **Photo card** — rounded corners, tape/frame, yellow location tag, source credit.
5. **Bar/progress chart** — 2–4 bars, counters, percentage labels.
6. **Comparison card** — A vs B, two photos/icons, red X / green check.
7. **Value-chain diagram** — 4–6 icons connected by arrows.
8. **Timeline** — horizontal line with dated dots.
9. **Quote/logo card** — official document, company logo, flag row.
10. **Outro/CTA card** — question + “comments / next episode”.

### Signature details
- Yellow tag labels with a location pin (e.g., “الناظور”).
- Red stamps for “wrong/cancelled” (“خطأ”, “ملغاة”).
- Hand-drawn circles, arrows, brackets, faint doodles in the background.
- Rounded white cards with thin outlines and soft shadows.
- Flags and company logos as small “proof” cards.
- The watermark “إنفوجرافيك عربي” + play icon.

## 6. Motion graphics rules

- **Sequential reveal:** headline → photo/card → label → number. Each element 0.2–0.6s,
  then hold.
- **Counters:** numbers count up 0 → target; bars fill 0 → target; the percentage label
  lands last.
- **Maps:** slow zoom/pan, route line draws, pins pop, labels appear one by one.
- **Photos:** slow Ken Burns zoom (3–8s), rounded corners, slide-in from the side.
- **Chapter cards:** wipe/slide up from the bottom, big number first, title second.
- **Transitions:** slide-up, fade, soft wipe; occasional green glitch effect for a topic
  change. Avoid hard cuts inside infographic scenes.
- **Diagrams:** shapes assemble piece by piece, arrows draw, labels arrive, small loops
  (smoke, pulse).
- **Documentary format:** real footage cuts every ~4s, slow zoom, lower thirds, map
  inserts.
- **Golden rule:** motion follows the sentence. Change the visual when the narrator
  starts a new idea, not on a fixed timer.

## 9. What style should the new channel use?

**Base template: Format A — clean mint + yellow (videos 3, 12, 13).**
Reasons:
- The cleanest and most recent-looking.
- Easiest to build and scale (few assets, reusable components).
- Best Arabic legibility and map/chart support.
- Works for economy, infrastructure, politics and social topics.

**Keep from the other formats:**
- Paper-style **chapter cards** and **yellow highlight/tag** language (format B).
- Documentary **map + footage** mode for big geopolitics stories (format D).
- The **“5 scenarios” / numbered chapter** structure.
- The **CTA outro** formula.

**Avoid as the main style:**
- Cartoon city/desert (expensive, less authoritative).
- Dark collage (too busy, harder to read).
- Pure dark vector (works only for technical explainers).

**Non-negotiables for consistency:**
1. Male Darija narrator, ~130 wpm, -14 LUFS.
2. Hook = date/number/place + stake in the first 10 seconds.
3. 3–6 numbered chapters.
4. One idea per screen, sequential reveal, big numbers, maps.
5. 2,200 words for a 15-minute video.
6. Outro: comment + two next-topic choices + like/share.
7. Bold Arabic type, mint/teal/yellow palette, source credit on every map/photo.
