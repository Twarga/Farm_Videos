## 3. The key comparison

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Remittances (MAD) | 118.9B | **122B** |
| Tourism receipts gross (MAD) | 114.5B | **138.6B** |
| Travel spending abroad (MAD) | 29.4B | **33.2B** |
| Net travel balance (MAD) | 85.1B | **105.4B** |

**The twist:** tourism's 138.6B is gross. After Moroccans' travel spending (33.2B),
the net travel balance is 105.4B — still **below** the 122B sent by MRE.

## 4. Caveats

- Tourism receipts include spending by Moroccans abroad when they visit Morocco.
  Some of that overlaps with the same diaspora that sends remittances. The two
  numbers measure different things.
- Remittances are transfers; they do not directly create jobs. Tourism is
  labor-intensive but seasonal and sometimes low-paid.
- The 2.5 million jobs figure is a ministerial statement, not a statistical count;
  the 827,000 direct jobs figure is more solid.
- The 6.1 million diaspora figure is the ministry's; the HCP census estimates 4.5M.
  Use "about 6 million" and note the difference.
