# style.md — production guide

The channel style we copy: «إنفوجرافيك عربي». This file is the rulebook for every
video. Read it before writing, designing or voicing anything.

---

## 1. Format

- 16:9, 1920×1080, 30fps.
- Length: 12–20 minutes (default 15).
- One topic per video. News hook first, context second, analysis last.
- Arabic, right-to-left. Darija narration + MSA technical words.

---

## 2. Script

### Length
- About **2,200 words** for 15 minutes (range 2,000–2,600).
- About **130 words per minute** when read aloud.
- Short sentences: 8–15 words.

### Structure
1. **Hook (0:00–0:20)** — a date, a number or a place + a surprising stake.
   Never start with a greeting.
2. **Context** — why now, what changed. Use “داكشي علاش” (that's why).
3. **3–6 numbered chapters** — each chapter: question → number/map/comparison →
   explanation → transition.
4. **Turn** — what it means for Moroccans: risks, scenarios, money, jobs.
5. **Close** — answer the opening question, then the CTA.

### Language rules
- Darija for the voice, MSA for facts and technical terms.
- Use: بزاف ديال، داكشي علاش، شنو مخبي، غادي، كيفاش، دابا، واش عارف، شكون اللي،
  علاش، فين، ماشي، راه، صافي.
- Connectors: لكن/ولكن، يعني، من بعد، حيث/حيت، باش، غير، حتى.
- Ask questions often: 1–2 per minute.
- Say numbers out loud and put them on screen.
- No long greetings, no filler, no repeated intro.

### Hook templates
1. “في [تاريخ]، [مكان] كان [رقم مفاجئ]. ولكن…”
2. “سنة [X] كان [رقم]، واليوم وصل [رقم]…”
3. “أول مرة في [مكان]: [إنجاز]…”
4. “بزاف كيظنو بلي [اعتقاد خاطئ]. لا، [الحقيقة]…”
5. “[سؤال]؟ الجواب أكبر مما كتظن.”
6. “[وثيقة/بلاغ] غادي يخرج [تاريخ] وغادي يقول…”

### Outro (always the same shape)
- Ask for an opinion in the comments.
- Offer **two** next-episode topics (A or B).
- Ask for like/share/subscribe.
- End with “نتلاقاو في حلقة جاية”.

## 3. Visual style

### Palette
- Background mint: `#D7E6E6` (light band `#BCD1D1`)
- Deep teal text: `#093532`
- Yellow accent: `#FDCA3B`
- Green: `#1C9639`
- Red: `#B71F22`
- Paper background (special episodes): `#EAE4D5`, teal `#0D7673`
- Dark documentary (special episodes): `#262B2E` + lime `#D5FE3D`

### Type
- Bold Arabic sans similar to Cairo / Tajawal / Almarai.
- Headline 60–90 px, body 36–48 px, source 20–24 px (at 1080p).
- Yellow highlight behind the key phrase only.
- Numbers big, in circles or boxes.

### Layout
- One idea per screen. 3–5 elements max.
- Section label top-right with a dot.
- Channel watermark top-left or bottom-left.
- Source credit at the bottom of every map or photo.
- Rounded white cards, thin outline, soft shadow.
- Photos with rounded corners, tape or a frame, yellow location tag.

### Scene types (reuse only these)
1. Hook/stat card.
2. Map scene (Morocco outline or satellite).
3. Chapter card (big number + title).
4. Photo card (rounded, tagged, sourced).
5. Bar/progress chart.
6. Comparison card (A vs B, red X / green check).
7. Value-chain diagram (4–6 icons + arrows).
8. Timeline with dated dots.
9. Quote/document/logo card.
10. Outro/CTA card.

### Signature details
- Yellow location tags with a pin.
- Red stamps for “wrong / cancelled”.
- Hand-drawn circles, arrows, brackets.
- Flags and company logos as small proof cards.
- Maps: Morocco green, rival country red/blue, route lines dashed.

---

## 4. Motion

- Reveal elements one by one: headline → photo/card → label → number.
  Each 0.2–0.6s, then hold.
- Numbers count up; bars fill; percentage lands last.
- Maps: slow zoom/pan, route draws, pins pop, labels one by one.
- Photos: slow zoom (3–8s), slide in from the side.
- Chapter cards: wipe/slide up, big number first.
- Transitions: slide, fade, soft wipe. Glitch only for a topic change.
- No hard cuts inside an infographic scene.
- Motion follows the sentence: change the visual when a new idea starts.

---

## 5. Voice and music

- Male narrator, Moroccan Darija, calm and explanatory.
- Pace ~130 words per minute.
- Loudness -14 LUFS, true peak about -1 dB.
- Music: subtle bed, about 20 dB below the voice, ducked, no lyrics.
- No long intro sting. Voice or image starts at 0:00.
- Almost no silence; cut dead air.

---

## 6. Titles and thumbnails

### Titles
- ~70–80 characters.
- Include a number/date, a stake and a “why now”.
- Use one of: سر / الحقيقة / شنو مخبي / أضخم / أخطر / لماذا / كيفاش / واش.
- Emoji only if it adds meaning.

### Thumbnails
- 2–4 lines of very bold Arabic.
- Yellow/green/red highlight boxes.
- One face or one dramatic object.
- Flags, map, arrows, circled object.
- A number in a colored box.
- Make 3 variants and A/B test them.

---

## 7. Do not

- Do not copy another channel's narration, footage, thumbnails or voice.
- Do not use formal MSA only — it must sound Moroccan.
- Do not publish automatically: human review before upload.
- Do not state a fact without a source; keep a source credit in the project.
- Do not use 4K; 1080p is enough.

---

## 8. Quality checklist (before render)

- [ ] Hook has a date/number/place in the first 10 seconds.
- [ ] 3–6 numbered chapters.
- [ ] About 2,200 words / 130 wpm.
- [ ] At least 8 questions in the script.
- [ ] At least one map, one chart and one number card.
- [ ] Every map/photo has a source credit.
- [ ] Outro asks for a comment + two next topics + like/share.
- [ ] Audio -14 LUFS, no clipping.
- [ ] Arabic text fits, no overflow, RTL correct.
- [ ] 3 thumbnail variants ready.
